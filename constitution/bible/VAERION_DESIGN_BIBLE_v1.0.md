## PART ZERO — What This Document Is

This Bible is the highest design authority of Vaerion. It defines what the
product must feel like to be true, the laws that make that feeling enforceable,
and the states in which truth is allowed to appear. Every section below it —
the Visual System, the Implementation Constitution, the registries, the
primitives, the gates — exists to satisfy this document and may never relax it.

Where this Bible is silent, the Visual System governs. Where both are silent,
the Implementation Constitution governs. Where all three are silent, engineers
stop and file an interpretation request (Implementation Constitution P-5).
Improvisation is never permitted, regardless of the quality of the result.

## PART ONE — The Emotional Core

**Vaerion feels like certainty without drama.**

The product's emotional positioning is fixed: *certainty without drama*. It is
calm the way a calibration laboratory is calm — not because nothing is at
stake, but because everything is measured. It never performs confidence. It
never performs urgency. It states what is known, shows how it is known, and
says out loud what is not known.

**The design language is named THE INSTRUMENT GRAMMAR.**

Vaerion is not styled like an app, a dashboard, or a marketing surface. It is
built like a precision instrument: a surface that renders measurements, verdicts,
and evidence. Every element either measures, indicates, or explains. An element
that does none of these does not exist.

**The emotional signature is CALIBRATED HONESTY.**

Every signal the interface emits is calibrated to the actual state of the
underlying fact. Nothing is rounded upward, softened, hidden, or exaggerated.
The interface's confidence is never higher than the evidence beneath it. When
the system does not know, the system looks like it does not know — and that
appearance is a feature, not a failure.

## PART TWO — The Essential Metaphor

**Vaerion is a metrology laboratory for machine claims — the verification
bureau for what agents assert.**

The governing metaphor is fixed and every design decision must remain coherent
with it: a metrology institute. Weights and measures carry seals because an
authority verified them, and the seal — not the weight — is what the world
trusts. Vaerion applies the same discipline to machine action: an agent's claim
is a measurement; the verdict is the seal; the receipt is the certified record;
the chain is the registry ledger.

Consequences that follow from the metaphor and are therefore law:

- Surfaces read like instruments, not like products. Hierarchy comes from
  measurement significance, never from decoration.
- The verdict seal — not the logo, not the headline — is the most important
  recurring visual object in the system.
- Numbers, hashes, timestamps, and versions are first-class visual citizens;
  they are never styled as filler.
- Ornament that a laboratory would not permit, the interface does not permit.

## PART THREE — The Four Verdicts

Exactly four verdict states exist. They are closed: no product surface may
introduce a fifth verdict, an alias, or a softer synonym. Each verdict has a
seal geometry, a meaning, and a prohibited misuse.

**VERIFIED — the solid seal.**
The claim was checked by a named verifier against a named rule set, and the
check holds. The seal is solid: full fill, full commitment. VERIFIED is the
only state permitted to carry affirmative trust color, and it never appears
without naming its verifier.

**UNVERIFIED — the hollow seal.**
No verification has been performed. This is not an error. It is not danger. It
is the honest default of a world where most claims have not yet been checked.
The seal is hollow — outline, no fill — because the system is deliberately
leaving the space empty rather than pretending it is full. UNVERIFIED is never
highlighted as a threat, never styled to alarm, and never quietly de-emphasized
into invisibility. It occupies its space at equal dignity.

**FAILED — the crossed seal.**
The claim was checked and did not hold. The seal carries the cross: the
verification was performed and produced refutation. FAILED always names what
failed and which verifier refuted it. It is precise, not emotional — a failed
measurement, not an accusation.

**PENDING — the pulse.**
Verification is in flight. PENDING renders as a pulse — the only element in
the entire system permitted to breathe — and it is never rounded forward to
VERIFIED, never silently dropped, and always cancellable with exact restoration
of the prior state.

**The seal shape system.**
Shape carries verdict meaning independently of color, so that the verdict
survives grayscale rendering, color-vision differences, and photocopy:

- Solid disc — VERIFIED.
- Hollow disc — UNVERIFIED.
- Crossed disc — FAILED.
- Pulse dot — PENDING.

Circles are reserved for truth: no decorative circle, dot, badge, or avatar may
imitate seal geometry anywhere in the product.

## PART FOUR — The Fourteen Articles

The Articles are immutable design law. They may be amended only through the
constitutional amendment pathway (Implementation Constitution 11.2–11.3), and
an amendment that relaxes one Article to serve another does not pass.

### Article I — No Borrowed Identity

Vaerion must never be mistakable for another product, another category, or
another era. It does not borrow the visual language of social platforms,
developer toys, or enterprise consoles. If a screen could be mistaken for a
different system, the screen is wrong. Identity is carried by the instrument
grammar itself: hairline structure, verdict seals, receipt composition, and
measured space. Imitation is not flattery; it is a verdict-integrity failure.

### Article II — Evidence or Silence

Every claim rendered in the interface carries either its evidence or an explicit
UNVERIFIED mark. There is no third option. The interface never states a fact it
cannot show the provenance of. Where evidence exists, it is reachable; where it
does not, the absence is declared, not disguised. Decorative assertions of
quality ("trusted", "secure", "verified" as ornament) are prohibited: a word
that asserts without evidence is rendered exactly like the unverified claim it
is — hollow.

Evidence is displayed with measurement honesty. A hash is shown with a
runcible discipline of its own: when a hash is displayed compactly it shows at
least ten characters, and any truncation is visibly marked — an elided hash is
labeled as elided, never presented as complete. The full value is never more
than one deliberate action away.

### Article III — A Verdict Names Its Verifier

No verdict exists without the identity of what produced it. Every VERIFIED,
FAILED, or PENDING state names its verifier: the engine version, the rule set,
and the environment in which the check ran. An anonymous verdict is
constitutionally void — the interface refuses to render trust that cannot be
attributed. This Article extends beyond runtime verdicts: every release of
Vaerion itself names the build engine and rule set that verified it. Audit
trust chains to their origin or do not display them at all.

### Article IV — Color Is Meaning

Color is a measurement channel, never decoration. Color is never the only
carrier of meaning: every color-coded state is simultaneously encoded by seal
shape, by label, and by position. If the chroma is removed, the verdict must
still read. Decorative color — gradients, accents, brand washes unrelated to
state — is prohibited on instrument surfaces. The total chromatic budget is
small by law and audited by the Visual System (§4.5): when everything is
highlighted, nothing is measured.

### Article V — Motion Is Information

Motion exists only to communicate state change. Every animation answers: what
changed, where, and in which direction of certainty. Motion is never
entertainment, never attention-seeking, never ambient. Duration is bounded at
400 milliseconds; anything slower is friction, anything instant is unperceived.
The system's three canonical motions — seal state change, sweep of a
verification pass, append of a new record — are the only permitted dramatic
acts. Reduced-motion preference receives full informational parity: nothing may
communicate through motion alone.

### Article VI — The Receipt Is Sacred

The receipt is the atomic unit of trust and its anatomy is fixed:

```
receipt id → claim → subject → verdict seal → verification method →
evidence[] → issued-at → chain parent
```

This sequence is the identity of the receipt object. Fields may be added; the
existing anatomy may never be reordered, merged, nested, or restyled out of
recognition. A receipt that cannot be recognized as a receipt across surfaces
— row, card, page, print, export — is a violation. The receipt never paginates
its own anatomy away, never hides the chain parent, and never renders a verdict
without its verification method.

### Article VII — Two Voices

The system speaks with exactly two voices and never blends them into a third.

**The Machine Voice** states measurements: identifiers, hashes, versions,
timestamps, counts, rule identifiers. It is set in a monospaced face, exact to
the character, and it never editorializes.

**The Human Voice** explains: what just happened, what a verdict means, what
the user may do next. It is set in a humanist sans face, plain and specific,
and it never markets. The two voices are visually distinct at all times — a
reader must be able to tell measurement from explanation without effort. The
Human Voice never restates a measurement incorrectly to sound friendlier; when
the two voices conflict, the Machine Voice is canonical.

### Article VIII — Honesty Over Comfort

When honesty and comfort conflict, honesty wins in every part of the interface.
Concretely and without exception:

- No optimistic verdicts. A claim is never rendered as verified while
  verification is pending, running, or assumed.
- UNVERIFIED is never styled as failure, and failure is never styled as noise.
- PENDING is shown, never hidden behind silence.
- Absence of evidence is rendered as absence, never as a reassuring placeholder.
- Empty states state the truth of the emptiness; loading states state what is
  loading and never imply completion.
- Counts, durations, and progress are real measurements or are not shown.

The interface is permitted to be disappointing. It is not permitted to be
misleading.

### Article IX — Chain Is Walkable

Every claim that inherits, derives, or depends on another claim renders its
chain position, and any link in that chain is reachable within two interactions
from the claim the user is viewing. The chain is never a dead end, never a
truncated ellipsis the user cannot open, and never a visual metaphor without
navigation. A broken chain is rendered honestly — with the break made visible
by the chainline break treatment — rather than silently re-rooted or skipped.

### Article X — The Ledger Renders Sliced

A ledger — any bounded record set: receipts, evidence items, events — renders
in slices, never as an unbounded wall. The interface always states its
measurement of the whole: "showing 24 of 1,412". Infinite-feeling scroll
without counts, totals without slices, and pagination that hides the total are
all violations, because each pretends a measurement that was not taken. The
sliced ledger keeps rows at the fixed ledger rhythm so that scanning is a
stable physical act.

### Article XI — Nothing Unmeasured Ships

Nothing ships without measurement and citation. A pixel, a color, a motion
curve, a copy string, a state, a rule: if it cannot be traced to an Article of
this Bible, a Section of the Visual System, or a rule of the Implementation
Constitution, it does not ship. Uncitabled work is not styled work in progress;
it is a violation. The enforcement instrument of this Article is the
Constitutional Trace Index (Implementation Constitution P-4).

### Article XII — The Proof Lens

The Proof Lens is the signature moment of the product — the single sanctioned
"wow": press and hold any claim and the entire interface recedes into a veil
while that claim's evidence chain illuminates in full ink. Everything unrelated
dims; everything relevant holds full contrast. Release, and the room restores.

The Lens is law in both directions: it must exist on every claim surface, and
it must obey the access-control law of the system — the Lens illuminates only
evidence the viewer is authorized to see, and it never widens visibility beyond
the underlying verification grant. The Lens obeys the motion law (Article V):
its transitions are ≤400 ms and fully mirrored under reduced motion. The Lens
is the reward for a quiet system; no other surface may compete for spectacle.

### Article XIII — Teach the States

The four verdicts are the product's alphabet, and the interface teaches them
permanently. Every verdict state carries, on first encounter and on demand
forever, a one-line plain-language explainer of what the state means. Explainers
never disappear permanently after a dismiss-once; a state legend remains
reachable from every surface that renders verdicts. The system never assumes
the user has learned the states, because a misread verdict is a failed verdict
surface. Teaching is done in the Human Voice (Article VII) and never in
marketing language.

### Article XIV — An Equal Instrument

The instrument is complete only if every operator can use it. The interface
conforms to WCAG 2.2 AA. Specifically and non-exhaustively: full keyboard
operation with visible focus; screen-reader parity for every verdict, seal,
hash, and chain; reduced-motion parity (Article V); grayscale survival
(Article IV); contrast conformance for text-level and glyph-level signals; and
touch targets that do not depend on pointer precision. Accessibility is not a
feature of the instrument; it is a property of being an instrument. A
capability that exists only for one class of perceiver is rendered as if it did
not exist.

## PART FIVE — The Signature Moment, Restated

The Proof Lens (Article XII) is the only spectacle the product permits. Its
power comes from contrast with everything else: a system that never dramatizes
earns one moment of controlled revelation. The Lens dims the room and lights
the proof. No gradient, glow, parallax, confetti, mascot, or marketing motion
may exist anywhere in the product, because each would spend the credibility the
Lens is built to deliver.

## PART SIX — The Brand Memory

The memory the product must leave is fixed in one sentence:

> **"Anything without proof says so out loud."**

UNVERIFIED visibility is the brand. A verification system that hides its
unverified claims is lying by layout, and a world in which the unverified are
quietly styled as fine is the exact failure Vaerion exists to end. Every
surface, every export, every print, every integrations surface carries this
doctrine: the hollow seal is not a gap in the product — it is the product's
signature of honesty. When a user remembers one thing about Vaerion, it is
that nothing unproven ever masqueraded as proven here.
